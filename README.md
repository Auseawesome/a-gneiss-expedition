## A dungeon crawler made for the Ember Jam by a team of 3.

- Play as a human spelunking in an overgrown dungeon and fight your way through hordes of Goblins to take their loot and use it to grow in power. Buy upgrades from the shop to prepare for your final battle against the Sorcerer and claim your victory!

- Press 1 and 2 to switch between sword and pickaxe. You can use this pickaxe to break through darker walls to open up areas of the map. Find the ladder to enter the next level.  Press the price buttons to buy items in the shop if you have enough money. Picking up health bushes will heal you slightly, though beware, some of them aren't quite as nice as they might originally seem...

- Features a timer and score system based on final money so you can compete the beat the game as quickly or as thoroughly as possible, or skip out on upgrades for a bigger challenge but higher score!

- All assets and code were hand-drawn/handwritten during the period of Ember specifically for this project and are available under Apache License 2.0 for anyone who would like to use them after this Jam is over
